# CS330-Comp-Graphic-Visualization

### How do I approach designing software?

When designing software, I like to start by understanding what I want the finished product to look like and then figure out how to bring everything together. For this project, I started with a 2D photograph of a house and worked on recreating it as a 3D scene using C++ and OpenGL. One of the biggest things I learned was how to take something from a picture and break it down into basic shapes that I could actually work with. I also became more comfortable with positioning objects, adjusting their sizes, and using textures and lighting to make everything look more realistic.

My design process involved focusing on the larger parts of the house first before moving on to smaller details like the windows, doors, porch, and surrounding environment. I found that planning things out this way made the project easier to manage. In the future, I can apply this same approach to other software projects by starting with the main requirements, breaking them into smaller tasks, and gradually improving the design as I go.

### How do I approach developing programs?

When developing programs, I usually try to get the main functionality working before spending too much time on the smaller details. Throughout this project, I learned how important it is to build and test things gradually instead of trying to complete everything at once. I worked with different 3D shapes, applied textures, adjusted lighting, and implemented camera controls that allowed users to move around the scene. I also organized the code into separate functions so I could make changes to individual objects without affecting everything else.

Iteration played a major role in completing this project because there were several times when I had to go back and adjust something that did not look the way I wanted. Whether it was changing the position of an object, fixing how a texture appeared, or adjusting the lighting, each change helped improve the scene. Looking back at the milestones, I can see how my approach to programming has improved. I became more comfortable working with C++ and OpenGL, troubleshooting problems, and understanding how different parts of a program work together to produce the final result.

### How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computer science has already helped me develop skills that I know will be useful as I continue my education and work toward becoming a software engineer. Before taking this course, I had experience with programming, but computational graphics introduced me to a different side of software development. Learning how 3D objects are created, how lighting affects their appearance, and how users interact with a virtual environment gave me a better understanding of what goes into developing graphical applications. I believe these skills will also be valuable as I continue my education because they have strengthened my problem-solving abilities and helped me become more comfortable learning unfamiliar technologies.

Professionally, I want to continue building software that people can actually use, and this project showed me another way programming can be applied to real-world problems. Even if I do not end up working directly with computer graphics, the experience I gained from organizing code, debugging, testing, and improving a project over time will carry over into other areas of software engineering. What I enjoyed most was seeing something that started as a simple photograph gradually become an interactive 3D environment. It reminded me that programming is not just about writing code, but also about using creativity and problem-solving to turn an idea into something functional.
